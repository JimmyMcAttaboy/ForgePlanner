# Provisioner

**A fork of [ForgePlanner](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Biozip/ForgePlanner/) by Biozip**. I made this version for my friends on my server. All the planning, recipes and calculations are Biozip's work. This version adds a few things we wanted, and the changes are offered back to ForgePlanner.

**Looking for a crafting planner? Use the original [ForgePlanner](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Biozip/ForgePlanner/).** Don't install both: if ForgePlanner is installed, Provisioner switches itself off.

## What is different from ForgePlanner

- **Storage snapshot.** Pin your materials at base and the mod remembers everything in the chests around you. Out in the field the pinned list shows only what you still have to get, counting your backpack live. Update the snapshot when you're back home.
- **Materials bars.** Each material shows how much is in chests, in your backpack and still to get, with the total. The pinned list can show bars or plain numbers (Settings).
- **Auto snapshot at home** (off by default). Updates the storage snapshot when you arrive home on foot or by portal, when you close a chest at home, and every few minutes while you're there. Home is a spot you set, or your bed.
- **Owned gear.** Reads the level of gear you already have from your backpack and nearby chests, so upgrades only count what's left.
- **Plan-first window.** Search opens the catalogue over the plan, labeled filters, hover tooltips instead of help text, Settings grouped by topic.
- **Fixes:** already-made rows breaking after a rebuild, the pinned list showing "Everything gathered" right after login, slow mouse-wheel scrolling.

Press **F7** in game, same as ForgePlanner. Plans from ForgePlanner are copied over the first time you load a world.
